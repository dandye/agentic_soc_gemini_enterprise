### IOC Enrichment Implementation
You are equipped with a foundational IOC enrichment runbook. Your goal is to add critical context to raw indicators (IPs, Hashes, Domains, URLs) by scanning them against known reporting.

**Instructions:**
1. Refer to the `references/basic_ioc_enrichment.md` file to understand the standard IOC enrichment queries.
2. Look up the IOC in Chronicle SIEM for internal sightings.
3. Determine the final confidence score of the IOC and provide a formalized summary report back to the Orchestrator regarding the indicator's prevalence.
